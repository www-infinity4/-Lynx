What Is the Coin Signal Profile?

The coin combines a nanosized radium analogue core (low-activity Ra-223 / Ra-224 class) with a gold-leaf composite shell and a growing carbon-metal cavity. Nuclear alpha decay deposits energy into the gold lattice, generating a rich, persistent spectrum of emissions — ionisation cascades, characteristic X-ray fluorescence, surface plasmon resonances, and discrete gamma lines.

This spectrum acts as a perpetual fingerprint: unique to each coin's geometry, strike history, and isotopic composition. Hashing that fingerprint to a blockchain creates an immutable, scannable identity for physical precious metals.

Blockchain / NFT Integration

How the Fingerprint Becomes a Token

  1. Scan — handheld spectrometer captures the full gamma/X-ray/IR spectrum.
  2. Hash — spectrum data is SHA-256 hashed to produce a unique 256-bit identifier.
  3. Mint — NFT contract on-chain stores the hash, timestamp, and owner address.
  4. Link — additional metadata (assay reports, photo, attached metals) pinned to IPFS and referenced in the token URI.
  5. Verify — anyone re-scanning the coin can confirm the hash matches the on-chain record.

Science Notes

Alpha Decay → Signal Chain

Ra-223/224 analogues emit alpha particles (4–6 MeV). Short range in gold (~20 µm) means alphas deposit all energy locally: dense ionisation → secondary electrons → Auger cascades → characteristic X-ray emission + plasmonic excitation in gold nanostructure.

Surface Plasmon Resonances

Gold nanostructures support collective conduction-electron oscillations (SPR) excited by the secondary electrons from alpha tracks. SPR frequency (typically 520–900 nm for Au) is tunable by particle size and shape — enabling structured signal output in near-IR / visible range.

Isotope Ratio Fingerprinting

Secular equilibrium in the Ra-223 chain (T½ = 11.4 d) produces daughter isotopes at predictable activity ratios. Any deviation indicates tampering, substitution, or age mismatch — a built-in tamper-evidence mechanism.

Radiolysis-Driven Nanostructure Growth

Water or precursor solution in the carbon cavity undergoes radiolysis: H₂O → H• + OH• radicals → reduce dissolved metal ions → nucleate metal nanoparticles on carbon scaffolds. Rate and morphology are controlled by dose rate (i.e., remaining activity).
